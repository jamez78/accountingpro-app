## AccountingPro ERP 5.10.0

A complete, offline accounting & ERP system built for Philippine businesses — by SakaynaTech Solution.

### ⬇️ Download
Download **`AccountingPro ERP Setup 5.10.0.exe`** below and run it. Windows 10/11 (64-bit). No other software required.

> Windows SmartScreen may warn that the publisher is unknown (the app is unsigned) — click **More info → Run anyway**.

### 🆓 Try it free — Demo Mode
With no license key, the app runs in **Demo Mode**: every feature is unlocked, limited to **20 records per module** and **1 user**. Enter a license key anytime to unlock the full version — **no reinstall needed**.

### ✨ What's included
- **Accounting** — General Ledger, AP, AR, journal entries, bank reconciliation, trial balance, financial reports
- **Philippine payroll** — SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG & BIR withholding tax auto-computed, with an approval workflow
- **Procurement** — Purchase Requisition → PO → Goods Receipt → Invoice Verification → Payment
- **HR & 201 File** — employee records, leave, attendance
- **37 modules · 16 business types** — a first-run Setup Wizard configures your chart of accounts and modules automatically
- **Standalone & offline** — runs as a Windows app; no internet required
- **LAN multi-user** — one PC hosts it; other office PCs connect by browser
- **Role-based security** — Admin, Accounting, HR, Sales, Purchasing

### 🚀 Getting started
1. Install and launch **AccountingPro ERP**.
2. Complete the Setup Wizard (company name + business type).
3. Log in with `admin` / `Admin@123` — then change the password.
